# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** 13 November 2026  
**Last Updated:** 13 November 2026

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how [Your Company Name], operating [Platform Name] ("we", "us", or "our"), collects, processes, stores and protects information obtained through our social media analytics platform.

Our platform is designed to analyse social media content, engagement metrics and performance data from Facebook and Instagram to improve marketing strategies, content recommendations and algorithmic optimisation.

We are committed to protecting personal information and processing data transparently, lawfully and responsibly.

## 2. Information We Collect

Depending on the permissions granted through Meta's developer platform, we may collect the following categories of information:

### A. Social Media Account Information
- Publicly available account information.
- Account names and usernames.
- Page and professional account identifiers.
- Account-level analytics made available through authorised API permissions.

### B. Content and Engagement Information
- Post and publication identifiers.
- Content publication dates and times.
- Engagement statistics, including likes, comments, shares and other available interactions.
- Reach, impressions and audience engagement metrics.
- Video performance and viewing statistics where available.
- Other analytical information provided through authorised Meta APIs.

### C. Technical Information
- API responses and associated metadata.
- Technical logs necessary to maintain the platform.
- Authentication and access-token information required to operate authorised integrations.

We only access information permitted by the relevant Meta API permissions and the applicable platform terms.

## 3. How We Use Information

Information collected through Facebook and Instagram is used for the following purposes:

1. **Performance Analytics:** To measure the performance of social media content and identify successful content strategies.

2. **Algorithm Optimisation:** To analyse engagement patterns, identify correlations and improve our internal content-ranking, recommendation and optimisation algorithms.

3. **Market Research:** To identify broader content trends, audience engagement patterns and changes in social media performance.

4. **Statistical Analysis:** To produce aggregated datasets, performance reports and analytical insights.

5. **Content Strategy:** To inform decisions regarding publication schedules, content formats, topics and marketing approaches.

6. **Platform Improvement:** To improve the functionality, reliability and analytical capabilities of our software.

7. **Research and Development:** To develop and refine analytical models and automated decision-support systems.

We do not use collected information for purposes unrelated to the operation and improvement of our platform without an appropriate legal basis.

## 4. Automated Processing and Artificial Intelligence

Our platform may use automated analytical systems, statistical models and artificial intelligence to evaluate social media performance.

These systems may:
- Identify patterns in content engagement.
- Compare the performance of different publication formats.
- Analyse relationships between publication timing and engagement.
- Identify emerging content trends.
- Generate recommendations for improving marketing performance.
- Refine internal algorithms based on historical performance data.

Automated analysis is intended to improve our understanding of social media performance and support marketing decisions.

We do not intend to use this information to make legally significant decisions about individuals.

## 5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell personal information or social media account data to third parties.

Information may be shared only where necessary for:
- Operating and maintaining our platform.
- Providing technical infrastructure and hosting.
- Meeting legal obligations.
- Protecting our legal rights and preventing misuse.
- Obtaining services from authorised processors operating under appropriate data protection agreements.

Where possible, analytical findings will be aggregated or otherwise de-identified before being used in reports or research.

We do not authorise third parties to independently exploit personal information obtained through our platform.

## 6. Data Storage and Security

We implement reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, loss or destruction.

These safeguards may include:
- Access controls and authentication.
- Encryption where appropriate.
- Secure storage of API credentials and access tokens.
- Restricted access to collected information.
- Regular review of data handling and security procedures.

No electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 7. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy.

Retention periods depend on the type of information, its intended use, applicable legal requirements and Meta's platform requirements.

Where information is no longer necessary, we will delete it, anonymise it or securely dispose of it, subject to applicable legal obligations.

Information obtained through Meta APIs will be retained and deleted in accordance with applicable Meta Platform Terms and Developer Policies.
